Expand description

Requires a ClaimAccount’s gas budget to cover the claim pipeline at the transaction’s own gas price and key size, so a claim that is accepted cannot run out of gas at execution. A no-op for every other transaction kind, and when the claim kind is disabled.

Reads the gas price and the gas budget, so a simulation can only run it once it has filled in the ones its caller left unset.
