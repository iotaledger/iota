Expand description

Whether a dry run without --gas-budget, taking balance from gas coins the caller gave, failed for lack of coin balance. An unset budget is filled in with the gas coins’ balance, up to the protocol maximum (max_tx_gas), which can leave too little to split from the gas coin.
