pub fn check_transactions_fit(
    transaction_sizes: &[u64],
    limits: BlockTransactionLimits,
) -> Result<(), BlockTransactionLimitError>
Expand description

Checks that Starfish accepts, in one block, transactions whose serialized sizes are transaction_sizes: there are no more of them than limits allows, each is no larger than the size limits allows for one transaction, and together they are no larger than the total size limits allows.

§Errors

BlockTransactionLimitError::TooManyTransactions when there are more transactions than the limit, and otherwise BlockTransactionLimitError::TransactionTooLarge or BlockTransactionLimitError::TooManyTransactionBytes for the first transaction that is over the size limit or takes the total over its limit. The total in BlockTransactionLimitError::TooManyTransactionBytes is the total up to and including that transaction, not the size of all the transactions.
