where Self: 'async_trait, 'life0: 'async_trait,

Fetches a list (sequence number, digest) pairs for transactions affecting the given address, ordered by TransactionsOrder and capped at limit.

§Pagination

cursor is exclusive.

  • TransactionsOrder::NewestFirst: returns entries with tx_seq < cursor.
  • TransactionsOrder::OldestFirst: returns entries with tx_seq > cursor.

When None, the scan starts from the beginning of the requested TransactionsOrder.
