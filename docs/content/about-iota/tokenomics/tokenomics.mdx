Tokenomics refers to the various concepts that shape the science and behavior of blockchain economies. In basic terms, **tokenomics** is the financial foundation of blockchains. Much the same way a building with a poor foundation is doomed to fail, a blockchain without a well-researched, extensively planned, and painstakingly implemented token economy eventually crumbles.

IOTA tokenomics is based on sound financial concepts confirmed by extensive blockchain research. Designed for scale, the IOTA tokenomics structure is intended to support the financial needs of Web3 now and in the future.

## The Actors in the IOTA Economy

Three main types of participants characterize the IOTA economy:

- **Users** submit transactions to the IOTA platform to create, mutate, and transfer digital assets or interact with more sophisticated applications enabled by smart contracts, interoperability, and composability.
- **Validators** manage transaction processing and execution on the IOTA platform.
- **IOTA token holders** have the option of delegating their tokens to validators and participating in the proof-of-stake mechanism. Owners of IOTA tokens also hold the rights to participate in IOTA governance.

## Tokenomics Overview

 The following flowchart provides a high-level overview of the monetary flow within the IOTA's tokenomics. At its core, the IOTA economy is driven by the [IOTA token](iota-token.mdx), the native asset of the IOTA platform.

Every epoch, IOTA tokens are minted and distributed to well-behaving validators who contribute to the system's security. A portion of these rewards is allocated to delegators after deducting the validator's commission.

When users submit transactions, they incur gas fees, a portion of which is burned to mitigate token inflation. Users can include an optional tip to incentivize validators for faster processing during network congestion. Additionally, some tokens are locked as a storage deposit when creating objects and are redeemable upon object deletion.

 



- **Value transfers:** The IOTA token enables efficient value transfers.
- **[Gas fees](gas-in-iota.mdx):** Gas fees are charged for all network operations, and consist of (i) _base computation fees_, which are burned, (ii) fully rebated _storage deposits_, and (iii) an _optional tip_, which is assigned to users participating in the delegated proof-of-stake mechanism, namely delegators and validators.
- **[Staking rewards](proof-of-stake.mdx):** Staking rewards are provided to delegators and validators. This incentivizes honest behavior by IOTA Validators and IOTA token holders that delegate to them.
- **Voting:** On-chain voting is used for governance and protocol upgrades.
