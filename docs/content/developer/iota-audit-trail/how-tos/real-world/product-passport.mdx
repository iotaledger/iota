# Digital Product Passport - Audit Trail

This real-world example demonstrates how to use IOTA Audit Trail to build the event history layer for a Digital Product Passport (DPP). The trail records every lifecycle event — from manufacturing through usage to end-of-life — in a tamper-proof, chronological log with role-based access control.

## Business Context

Regulations such as the EU Digital Product Passport mandate that products carry a verifiable history of their lifecycle. This includes manufacturing conditions, quality inspections, ownership transfers, maintenance events, and disposal records. A blockchain-based audit trail provides:

- **Regulatory Compliance**: Verifiable, tamper-proof records that satisfy data retention requirements
- **Supply Chain Transparency**: Every stakeholder can independently verify the product's history
- **Consumer Trust**: End users can verify authenticity and sustainability claims
- **Circular Economy**: Recyclers and refurbishers have access to the complete material composition and maintenance history

## Field Usage Strategy

The Audit Trail's features map directly to DPP requirements:

- **`immutable_metadata`**: Product identity — name, model, serial number, manufacturer
- **`updatable_metadata`**: Current status summary (e.g., `"Status: In Service | Owner: Fleet Corp"`)
- **`record.data`**: Event payload — either a hash of the off-chain event document or the full event as a JSON string
- **`record.metadata`**: Structured event context (e.g., `"event:maintenance;type:battery_replacement"`)
- **`record.tag`**: Event category — used with RoleTags to enforce that only the right party can record each event type

### Role Design

| Role | Permissions | RoleTags | Holder |
|------|------------|----------|--------|
| `Admin` | Full administrative control | — | Product manufacturer |
| `ManufacturingRecorder` | AddRecord | `"manufacturing"` | Factory systems |
| `QualityInspector` | AddRecord | `"inspection"` | QA department |
| `MaintenanceRecorder` | AddRecord, CorrectRecord | `"maintenance"` | Authorized service partners |
| `TransferAgent` | AddRecord | `"transfer"` | Logistics providers |
| `Auditor` | *(read-only)* | — | Regulators, consumers |

## Real-World Applications

### Electric Vehicle Batteries

- **Scenario**: Tracking battery lifecycle from cell manufacturing through vehicle integration, usage, and recycling
- **Tags**: `"manufacturing"`, `"assembly"`, `"charge_cycle"`, `"maintenance"`, `"recycling"`
- **Benefits**: Satisfies EU Battery Regulation requirements for battery passports

### Pharmaceutical Supply Chain

- **Scenario**: Recording drug manufacturing, quality testing, distribution, and dispensing
- **Tags**: `"manufacturing"`, `"quality_control"`, `"distribution"`, `"dispensing"`
- **Benefits**: EU Falsified Medicines Directive compliance, anti-counterfeiting

### Luxury Goods Authentication

- **Scenario**: Provenance tracking for high-value goods from creation to resale
- **Tags**: `"creation"`, `"certification"`, `"sale"`, `"authentication"`
- **Benefits**: Anti-counterfeiting, resale value preservation, authenticity verification

### Industrial Equipment

- **Scenario**: Maintenance and inspection logging for machinery and infrastructure
- **Tags**: `"installation"`, `"inspection"`, `"maintenance"`, `"incident"`, `"decommission"`
- **Benefits**: Regulatory compliance, predictive maintenance, safety auditing

## Data Structure Example

### Trail Metadata

```
Immutable: "EV Battery Pack BP-2026-0042 | Manufacturer: CellTech GmbH | Model: CT-100kWh"
Updatable: "Status: In Service | Vehicle: VIN-WBA12345 | Owner: Fleet Corp"
```

### Sample Records

| Seq | Tag | Data | Metadata | Added By |
|-----|-----|------|----------|----------|
| 0 | `manufacturing` | `"Cell assembly completed. 96 cells, 100.2 kWh nominal"` | `"event:assembly_complete;batch:B-2026-Q1-042"` | Factory |
| 1 | `inspection` | `"QA passed. Capacity: 100.1 kWh. Internal resistance: nominal"` | `"event:qa_passed;inspector:QA-007"` | QA Dept |
| 2 | `transfer` | `"Shipped to assembly plant. Carrier: LogiCorp #LC-5521"` | `"event:shipment;destination:plant-munich"` | LogiCorp |
| 3 | `maintenance` | `"Firmware update v2.1.0 applied. No anomalies detected"` | `"event:firmware_update;technician:ST-042"` | Service Partner |
